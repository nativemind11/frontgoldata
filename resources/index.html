Glossary

Active learning
Selecting the most informative unlabeled items for humans to label next.
Annotation
Adding labels or structure to raw data so a model can learn from it.
ASR
Automatic speech recognition: converting speech audio into text.
Benchmark
A fixed dataset and procedure used to compare models consistently.
Bounding box
A rectangle drawn around an object in an image or video frame.
Calibration round
A session where raters label the same items and align on disagreements.
Clean read
A transcript style that removes hesitations and false starts while keeping meaning.
Code-switching
Moving between two or more languages within one conversation or sentence.
Cohen's kappa
An agreement score for two raters that corrects for chance agreement.
Consensus labeling
Combining several independent judgments into one final label.
Consent record
Documentation of what a contributor agreed to and how their data may be used.
Data card
A short document describing a dataset's origin, composition, intended use and limits.
Data leakage
When evaluation data also appears in training data, inflating results.
Diarization
Segmenting audio by speaker to show who spoke when.
Dialect
A regional or social variety of a language with its own vocabulary or pronunciation.
Edge case
An unusual item the guideline does not clearly cover.
Gold item
A reference item with a verified answer used to measure accuracy.
Guideline
The written rules annotators follow to make consistent decisions.
Hallucination
A model output that is fluent but not supported by facts or input.
Human in the loop
A workflow where people review, correct or guide automated output.
Inter-annotator agreement
How often independent annotators give the same answer to the same item.
Instruction tuning
Fine-tuning a model on prompt and response pairs so it follows instructions.
Intent
The goal behind a user's request, such as booking, cancelling or asking.
Keypoint
A labelled point on an object, such as a joint on a human pose.
Krippendorff's alpha
An agreement measure for any number of raters, tolerant of missing data.
Locale
A language plus region combination, such as Arabic (Egypt) or French (Canada).
Low-resource language
A language with little digital text, audio or tooling available.
LQA
Linguistic quality assurance: native review of translated or localized content.
Named entity
A real-world item in text, such as a person, place, organization or date.
Orthography
The agreed spelling and writing conventions of a language.
Pilot
A small batch produced under real conditions to prove fit before scaling.
Polygon
A many-sided shape traced tightly around an object's outline.
Preference data
Pairs or rankings of model outputs judged by people, used to train reward models.
Prompt
The input text or instruction given to a generative model.
Quota
A target count for a group in a dataset, such as speakers per dialect.
Red-teaming
Deliberately probing a model to find unsafe or unwanted behavior.
RLHF
Reinforcement learning from human feedback: training models using human preferences.
Rubric
A scoring guide that defines what each rating level means.
Search relevance
How well a search result answers the intent of a query.
Segmentation
Dividing data into meaningful parts, such as pixels by class or audio by utterance.
SFT
Supervised fine-tuning on examples of desired model behavior.
Style guide
Conventions for formatting, terminology and tone in transcripts or translations.
Transcreation
Re-creating content for another culture rather than translating it literally.
Verbatim
A transcript style that keeps every hesitation, repetition and filler word.
Wake word
A short phrase that activates a voice assistant.
WER
Word error rate: the share of words a speech recognizer gets wrong.

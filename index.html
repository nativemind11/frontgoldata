Smart data. Human expertise.

AI teams need data that reflects the people who will use their systems. GOLDATA plans multilingual collection, annotation, localization and evaluation programs around a written brief. Native-language contributors complete the work; calibrated review checks it against agreed instructions; and delivery includes the files and documentation the client has approved.

11 disciplines.

  • Speech & Voice — Plan consented scripted or conversational speech around the devices, environments and language varieties your project requires. Depending on scope, delivery can include recordings, transcripts, speaker turns, timestamps and reviewed audio metadata.
  • Data Collection — Collect text, audio, image or video to a written specification. Define contributor instructions, consent, locale quotas, capture conditions and metadata before production begins.
  • Data Annotation — Turn raw inputs into structured labels for text, image, speech and video. Start with a task schema, examples and rules for ambiguous items; use a pilot batch to align contributors before scaling.
  • AI Model Evaluation — Use a written rubric and calibrated reviewers to assess model responses for task fit, language quality, instruction following and agreed safety dimensions. Report the sample, criteria and limitations alongside the findings.
  • Quality Assurance — Plan quality before production with calibration examples, reference items, layered review and an issue taxonomy. Agree sampling and acceptance thresholds with the client; deliver a report that separates accepted items from open questions.
  • Industry Solutions — Scope data workflows for sectors such as automotive, healthcare, finance, customer support and retail. Confirm specialist qualifications, client terminology and applicable handling rules before the project begins.
  • Translation & Localization — Adapt prompts, datasets and product language for an agreed locale. A project can include glossary setup, native review, tone and intent checks, terminology decisions and linguistic QA.
  • Generative AI Services — Support LLM and multimodal programs with scoped preference judgments, supervised examples, prompt evaluation and red-team tasks. Define the policy and escalation rules with the client before rating begins.
  • Off-the-Shelf Datasets — Where a suitable catalog item exists, provide a dataset card describing source, license, permitted use, locale, schema, known limitations and last review date. Confirm catalog availability and rights before promising delivery.
  • Data Evaluation — Review dataset coverage, format, labels, linguistic quality, duplicates or agreed risk areas. Share the sampling method and documented issues so the client can decide what needs remediation.
  • Transcription — Prepare audio, video or image-derived text according to the selected transcript style. Scope speaker labeling, timestamps, non-speech events, language switching, uncertainty marks, file formats and review depth in the project brief.

FAQ

Which languages and dialects do you cover?

We build teams around your target locales, including regional dialects and lower-resource languages. Share your list and we confirm coverage, contributor availability and timelines before any work starts.

How do you keep our data secure?

Access is limited to the people who need it, contributors sign confidentiality agreements, and we agree storage, transfer and deletion rules with you before any data moves. We can work inside your environment when required.

How do you control quality?

Contributors are qualified and calibrated against gold examples, work passes through layered review, and accuracy is measured continuously. You see quality reports throughout the project, not just at the end.

What is a typical turnaround?

It depends on volume, languages and complexity. After scoping we give you a written schedule with milestones; pilots typically come first so the timeline rests on real throughput.

Can we start with a pilot?

Yes, and we recommend it. A pilot aligns guidelines and quality targets on a small batch before you commit to full volume.

How is pricing structured?

Per unit (hour of audio, item, label or word), per evaluator hour, or a fixed project fee — whichever fits the work. Every quote is itemized so you know exactly what you pay for.

Which tools do you work with?

Your own platform, common open-source annotation tools, or a setup we configure for the project. Delivery formats follow your pipeline.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA before scoping, or provide ours. Contributor confidentiality agreements apply to every project.

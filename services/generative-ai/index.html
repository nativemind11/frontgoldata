FAQ

Can you red-team in languages other than English?

Yes. Multilingual red-teaming is a core reason teams come to us: failure modes differ by language and culture.

Do your writers have domain expertise?

We staff by task. For specialist domains we recruit and test contributors with relevant background.

How is sensitive red-team content handled?

Under agreed wellbeing and access protocols, with restricted teams and secure storage.

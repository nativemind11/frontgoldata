FAQ

Yes. We sample, review, report and, if you want, correct the dataset.

What accuracy target do you work to?

The one we agree with you. We set acceptance criteria per task before work begins and report against them.

Do we see quality results during the project?

Yes. You receive regular quality reports, not just a final summary.

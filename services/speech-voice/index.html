What we do

We design recording programs around the way people really speak: regional accents, mixed languages, background noise, hesitations and all. Native-speaker contributors record to your script or speak freely, and trained transcribers turn every clip into clean, time-aligned text with speaker turns and event tags.

FAQ

Can you record in specific acoustic environments?

Yes. We plan sessions for quiet rooms, home settings, vehicles, outdoor spaces or call-center lines, and document the setup for every file.

Do you support code-switching?

Yes. We recruit speakers who naturally mix languages and write guidelines so transcribers tag every switch consistently.

Which transcription formats do you deliver?

Whatever your pipeline ingests: JSON, CSV, TextGrid, SRT, VTT or a custom schema we agree on up front.
